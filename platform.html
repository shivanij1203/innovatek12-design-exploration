Where we started

Strategic planning is still the heart of this work.

We bring students, staff, families, and partners into the conversation, then turn what they say into a plan with clear goals, owners, and timelines. That is where every plan starts, and it has not changed.

Where we are now

A plan only works if it stays live.

The platform grew out of that need. It holds district data in one place and keeps the goals from your plan in front of your team all year, so when someone asks how a goal is going, the answer is already there.

Capabilities

Three ways to read the same district data

AI Assistant

Ask in plain language. You get a direct answer with the evidence attached, so you can check the source before you act.

Dashboards

See where things stand. One view for the measures your leadership team tracks.

Scorecards

Track the goals in your plan. Each goal carries its own measures, so you can tell progress from activity.

All three read from the same district data, so the dashboard, the scorecard, and the assistant never disagree.

How an answer gets built

1

Ask in plain language

You type the question the way you would say it out loud.

2

Read from governed district data

The answer draws only on district data that has been approved for use.

3

Answer with sources attached

You get the answer and the records behind it together, so you can check before you act.
